Flagship build

The build nobody asked for

I was hired as an executive assistant. I saw a reporting gap, so I built the tool to close it.

The problem

The Sales Performance Coach at the #1 Allstate agency in the US had agent numbers spread across sheets, with no single place to see who was on track and who needed coaching.

What I built

A custom web app: a Google Sheets + Apps Script backend, login, live Chart.js dashboards, KPI and tier-ranking logic, and an agent leaderboard.

The result

Warm transfers, quote rates, close rates, and 60-day trends for about 60 agents in one view. Week by week, the trend is obvious at a glance, and the weekly and monthly reporting updates itself.

How it works

From idea to running, without the back-and-forth

Most builds follow the same four steps. You stay in the loop the whole way, and you never have to learn the tools yourself.

1

Free 30-minute call

Walk me through your event, funnel, or reporting headache. I ask about your tools, your audience, and what “done” looks like.

2

A scoped plan

You get a short written plan: the pages, workflows, and integrations, plus what I need from you. Scope is agreed before any work starts.

3

Build and test

I build it in your accounts, run test signups and orders end to end, and walk you through it before anything goes live.

4

Handoff with an SOP

You get a written SOP so your team can run it, duplicate it for the next event, or tweak it without waiting on me.
